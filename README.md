# Privacy Policy for Spook Monke Horror

Last updated: 10/7/2026

Summary: Spook Monke Horror does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Any mention of We Us / Our Refers to Jamtime Studios

Data collection: The game does not collect personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or device identifiers. It has no accounts, no ads, and no third-party tracking or analytics services.

Data stored on your device: Settings and save progress are stored locally on your headset and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Third parties: We don't share data with third parties because we don't have any. Meta processes data as part of running the Quest platform and store, under its own privacy policy: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/

Children: Because we collect no data, we collect none from children either.

Changes: If this ever changes, we'll update this policy and the "last updated" date.
